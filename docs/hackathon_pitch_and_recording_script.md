## Part 1: Hackathon Pitch Deck (The Slide Deck)

### Slide 1: Title & Vision
- **Title**: Project Drishti — Next-Generation Police Crime Intelligence Platform
- **Tagline**: Empowering Karnataka State Police with AI-Driven Biometrics, Dual English & Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) Voice Intelligence, and Multi-Tier Hierarchy Control
- **Presenter**: Team Drishti

---

### Slide 2: The Problem Statement
- **Fragmented Data**: Crime records, suspect mugshots, and case files are isolated across different police stations.
- **Language Barrier**: Need for native Kannada voice speech and UI support for beat officers and investigating staff.
- **Biometric Delays**: Manual mugshot matching slows down suspect identification during critical investigation hours.
- **Role & Account Governance**: Lack of real-time administrative control over officer access and range-wide oversight.

---

### Slide 3: The Solution — Project Drishti
- **Bilingual English <-> Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) UI**: Full website localization with 1-click header language toggle.
- **Drishti AI Dual Voice Speech Engine**: Real-time microphone listening and spoken Kannada AI voice synthesis (`kn-IN`).
- **Biometric Facial Recognition**: Instant suspect mugshot identification via uploaded photos against criminal lineup databases.
- **Police Hierarchy Control**: DSP Superintendent oversight with full Range-wide case access and instant account activation/suspension.
- **AI Graph & One-Click Data Exports**: Graph traversal for hidden suspect connections and 1-click CSV/TXT exports across all modules.
